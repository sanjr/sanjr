Privacy Policy

Last updated:

COPA MCQ ("we", "our", "the website") is a free online learning platform for ITI Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) trainees. This policy explains what information is used when you visit the website.

1. Information we collect

  • Student account: when you register we store your name, roll number, batch and a scrambled (hashed) 4-digit PIN. This is used only to show your own dashboard and results.
  • Test results and practice progress: scores, dates, modules attempted and time taken.
  • Live exam status: while you take a CBT mock test, your name, roll number, test name and progress may be shown to the site administrator (your instructor) for monitoring.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, e-mail address, Aadhaar, address or payment details.

2. Where the data is stored

Data is stored in your browser (localStorage) and, when the online admin feature is enabled, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which is operated by Google. Only the site administrator can read results and student lists.

3. Cookies and advertising

  • This website may show advertisements served by Google AdSense.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. How we use the information

Only to run the website: show your results, let instructors track class progress, improve the question bank, and show advertising that keeps the site free. We never sell personal information.

5. Children

The website is meant for ITI trainees. If you are under 18, use the website with the permission of a parent, guardian or instructor.

6. Your choices

You can log out at any time. Clearing your browser data removes the information stored on your device. To delete results stored online, contact the administrator.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

For any privacy question, see the Contact section.
