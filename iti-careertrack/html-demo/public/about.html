What CareerTrack does

  • Gives every student one permanent Career ID (PCID) that never changes, even after a transfer.
  • Keeps the full history: admission, attendance, modules, assessments, every exam attempt.
  • Records every apprenticeship, job, business and course as a separate record — nothing is overwritten.
  • Separates verified from pending information, and never treats “not updated” as unemployed.
  • Shows each person only what their role allows, and keeps an audit trail of sensitive changes.

Privacy

Personal details (phone, address, documents, salary) are visible only to authorised staff of the student's own ITI and to the student. Employers see a candidate only after the candidate applies and agrees to share their profile. The full Aadhaar number is never stored. Public pages show only overall numbers.
