Contact & help

Who to contact for what.

Students & alumni

Forgot your password, or a detail on your Career Passport is wrong? Contact the office of your ITI. You can also submit an update from Update Status after logging in.

ITI staff

Account or role problems: contact your Principal. Principal accounts are managed by the District / State office.

Employers

To register your company or hire ITI trainees, contact the Placement Officer of the nearest ITI.

Official contact details

The official helpline, e-mail and office address will be published here after approval by the department. This is a DEMO build.
