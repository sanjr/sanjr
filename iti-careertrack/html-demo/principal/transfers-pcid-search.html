How a transfer works

  • 1. Origin ITI requests
    With reason and effective date. Only one open request per student.
  • 2. Destination ITI reviews
    Sees only name, PCID, trade and reason until it accepts.
  • 3. Accept = one safe step
    Old admission closed (kept), new admission opened, old teachers ended, PCID unchanged.
  • 4. Access recalculated
    New ITI sees full history; old ITI keeps only its own records.
