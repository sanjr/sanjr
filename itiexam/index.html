Free ITI Exam Preparation for All Trades

ITIExam.com is a free online platform for ITI students preparing for the NCVT / SCVT ITI CBT exam. Practise trade-wise ITI mock tests, solve ITI previous year question papers with answers, and revise with module-wise notes and MCQ question banks for Trade Theory, Employability Skills, Workshop Calculation & Science and Engineering Drawing.

Whether you are in COPA, Electrician, Fitter, Turner, Welder, MMV, Plumber, Wireman, Machinist or Diesel Mechanic, you get exam-pattern questions for 1st year and 2nd year, along with the latest ITI jobs, apprenticeship (NAPS / NATS) openings, admit card and ITI result updates, including Maharashtra DVET.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are ITI mock tests on ITIExam.com free?

Yes. All ITI mock tests, previous year papers and study material are 100% free for students of every trade.

Is the ITI exam conducted in CBT mode?

Yes. NCVT ITI theory, Employability Skills and Workshop Calculation & Science exams are Computer Based Tests with MCQs. Our mock tests follow the same pattern.

Which ITI trades are covered?

30+ trades including COPA, Electrician, Fitter, Turner, Welder, MMV, Plumber, Wireman, Machinist, Diesel Mechanic and more, for 1st and 2nd year.

How can I apply for ITI apprenticeship?

Register on the official Apprenticeship India portal (NAPS). Our step-by-step guide covers registration, eligibility, documents and company-wise openings.
